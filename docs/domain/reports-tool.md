# Reports tool

The Reports tool finds and reads durable scout reports.

## Finding reports

Reports from registered-project and independent research are listed together, newest first. A project filter limits the list to that project. Independent research is identified as `_independent`.

## Reading reports

A listed report can be read in full using its listed name. When two projects hold reports with the same name, prefix the name with its project or `_independent`. Reports remain available after their scout completes.
